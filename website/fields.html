Field catalog · not the national file

US crop fields
by state and crop

California blocks are surveyed DWR 2023 fields, still grouped by county. The other states are a hangar sample of USDA 2024 crop-sequence polygons: Iowa, Minnesota, and the Dakotas from the public map service, plus Arkansas rice, Georgia peanuts, Illinois corn and soy, Kansas winter wheat, Nebraska corn, and Texas cotton from the national file. That file stays out of the site. Orchard blocks get rows along the long axis. Corn, soy, wheat, cotton, rice, and peanuts keep an open-field sweep. Check several blocks to plan them as one ranch.

Not a flight clearance. These boundaries are a public sample. Paths still ignore wind, wires, terrain, and other aircraft. Read /legal before you arm anything.
